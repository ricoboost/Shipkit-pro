# ShipKit Marketing - Content Generation Skill

Generate marketing content for SaaS products. Includes landing page copy, email sequences, social media content, and blog posts.

## Capabilities

### Landing Page Copy
- Hero headlines and subheadlines
- Feature descriptions
- Pricing page content
- CTA text and microcopy

### Email Marketing
- Welcome sequences
- Onboarding emails
- Upgrade/upsell campaigns
- Churn prevention

### Social Media
- Launch announcements
- Feature releases
- Testimonial requests
- Engagement posts

### Blog Content
- Comparison articles
- Tutorial posts
- Case studies

## Brand Voice

ShipKit Pro brand guidelines:

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Tone | Professional, confident, helpful |
| Voice | Clear, direct, technical but approachable |
| Audience | Developers, founders, technical teams |
| Values | Speed, quality, reliability, flexibility |

## Headline Formulas

### Value-focused
- "Ship your SaaS [X]% faster with [product]"
- "The [adjective] way to [benefit]"
- "Stop [pain point]. Start [benefit]."

### Feature-focused
- "[Feature] that [benefit]"
- "The only [product type] with [unique feature]"
- "[Number]+ [things] to help you [outcome]"

### Social proof
- "Trusted by [number] [audience]"
- "Join [number] developers who [action]"
- "The [category] used by [notable companies/people]"

## Email Subject Line Formulas

- "Welcome to [product] - here's what's next"
- "[First name], your [product] setup is almost complete"
- "You're missing out on [feature/benefit]"
- "We noticed you haven't [action] yet"
- "Quick question about your experience"

## CTA Text Patterns

### Primary CTAs
- "Get Started" / "Start Free" / "Try Free"
- "Get [Product]" / "Buy Now"
- "Start Building" / "Start Shipping"

### Secondary CTAs
- "Learn More" / "See How It Works"
- "View Demo" / "Watch Video"
- "See Pricing" / "Compare Plans"
